## Inspiration
Notebooks, voice memos and half-finished ideas end up scattered and unused, and few people want to put their inner life into a cloud app. With a background in psychology and coaching, I wanted a private place where journaling leads somewhere: to clearer thinking, braver steps and practical results.

## What it does
Storytime is a private journal and coach that runs on the UGREEN AI NAS. You write, speak or photograph an entry, then choose how you want help: Listen, Explore, or Act or create. You follow self-paced programmes with your own goal and time frame, try small experiments with review dates, ask "Talk with my diary" questions that are answered with sources, and get a coach's view of your patterns. It can also turn your real experience into outputs such as an achievement bank, a job application kit and CV drafts. Everything stays on your NAS.

## How we built it
I started from my own notes, sorted them into journeys and modules, and made a concept mockup to test the experience. I then wrote the Skill with the UGREEN Skill Developer Package: an orchestration SKILL.md that hands each task to the NAS experts (file, document, knowledge and photo agents), plus 12 reference files covering journeys, privacy, integrity and support boundaries. It passes the kit's static checker. Claude helped with structuring, drafting and quality checks.

## Challenges we ran into
- Fitting a big idea into what a Skill can do in a text chat. The visual design became the target for a future companion app.
- Matching every step to a real NAS capability, and being honest about what isn't there yet: reminders, encryption, sharing with named people.
- Designing for accuracy: telling recorded facts apart from interpretation, and never inventing achievements or dates.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of
- A coach that respects the user: "Just save this", "Don't analyse this" and "Stop here" always work, and plot twists are invitations only.
- Diary answers that show the entries they come from, label interpretations, and admit gaps.
- An application kit that connects the job ad, the role description, LinkedIn text and your own evidence, with gaps kept in brackets instead of filled in.

## What we learned
Trust comes from small, honest details: showing sources, asking before sharing, and keeping the user in control of how deep a conversation goes. A clear scope for version 1 beats a long feature list.
