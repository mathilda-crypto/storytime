# Storytime – user guide

**Storytime – Connect your dreams** is a private journal and coach that lives on your own NAS.

## Getting started

Say "start Storytime". You will be asked for a folder for your journal and three preferences: tone, plot twists and how often you want to check in. You can change them any time.

## Moving around

Jump between anything at any time. Say **"menu"** to see all options, or **"where was I?"** to continue where you left off – even after using other things on your NAS. Questions about bank statements or bills go to the Private Spend Compass Skill, if you have it.

## Writing

- Type, send a voice note, or photograph a handwritten page.
- Choose how you want help: **Listen**, **Explore**, or **Act or create**.
- Say **"Just save this"**, **"Don't analyse this"**, **"Skip this topic"** or **"Stop here"** whenever you like.

## Programmes

Pick one or more journeys and one main focus, write your goal in your own words, and choose a time frame – 7, 30 or 90 days, your own number, or open-ended. Check-ins show your day, your current small experiment and, if you like, a thought for the day. Pause, skip or go lighter without catching up. Ask "show my map" for an overview.

## Asking your diary and your coach

- "Talk with my diary: when did I start the pottery class?" – answers with the entries they come from, and what is missing.
- "Talk with my coach: what patterns do you see?" – suggestions linked to your goals, always marked as suggestions.

## Job applications

Save the job ad and role description in a folder, paste your LinkedIn text, and say "help me prepare this application". You get matches with your own evidence, honest gaps, and drafts for your CV, cover letter and LinkedIn. Storytime never posts or applies for you.

## Your data

Entries are plain files in your folder. Nothing is shared unless you choose it and see a preview first. Private vault entries are never used unless you ask.

Storytime is a coach, not therapy. If you are struggling or feel unsafe, contact local emergency services, a crisis line or someone you trust.
