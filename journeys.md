# Journeys

A person can follow several journeys, with one main focus at a time. In version 1 every journey uses the same modes, programmes, check-ins and diary questions. The **Career and freelance** journey also has dedicated outputs ([Application kit](application-kit.md)). Dedicated outputs for the other journeys are on the roadmap.

| Journey | Focus | Outputs (v1 = conversation and saved entries; roadmap = dedicated output) |
|---|---|---|
| Career and freelance | Identify strengths, collect evidence of achievements, prepare CVs and applications, practise interviews | v1: achievement bank, application kit, tailored CV, cover letter and LinkedIn drafts, application plan |
| Relationships and finding a partner | Explore needs, boundaries, communication and recurring experiences | v1: a conversation draft, clearer intentions, a small relationship action. Scope: reflection and profile wording, not matchmaking |
| Money and finances | Relationship with money, goals, money conversations, calm routines | v1: money reflection entries, a savings goal or challenge as an experiment, a conversation draft. Never invents figures; not financial advice. For statements and bills, suggest the separate Private Spend Compass Skill |
| Personal planning and habits | Connect reflections to priorities, routines and goals | v1: a realistic weekly plan and a habit experiment |
| Learning and getting better | Define a skill, practise deliberately, reflect on progress | v1: a learning plan and practice journal entries |
| Creativity and expression | Capture ideas; explore writing, art, music and everyday problems | v1: prompts, story concepts, creative exercises |
| Dreams and inner life | Dreams, symbols, beliefs, spirituality and questions, privately | v1: a personal meaning journal, saved in the vault by default. Symbols are personal possibilities, never fixed meanings, supernatural claims or diagnoses |
| Legacy and family | Preserve memories, recipes, photographs and stories | v1: memory entries, photo albums of confirmed photos, story drafts. Roadmap: family book layout, printable calendar, film outline |
| Later option: Food tracking and recipes | Food log and recipe collection | Roadmap |

## Goal ideas the user can pick or rewrite

- Update my CV and apply for five roles.
- Be braver – try one new thing a week.
- Have one honest conversation I have been avoiding.
- Practise a skill 20 minutes, four days a week.
- Write down ten family stories before the holidays.
