# Programmes and check-ins

## A programme

- **Time frame** – 7, 30 or 90 days, a custom number of days, or open-ended. Default 30.
- **Journeys** – any number; exactly one is the **main focus**. The others stay in the background and can be made the main focus at any time.
- **Goal** – one sentence in the user's own words, for example "Update my CV and apply for five roles" or "Be braver – try one new thing a week".
- **Phases** (optional, shown on the map) – for 30 days: Explore (week 1), Prepare and apply / Practise (weeks 2–4), Finish (week 5). Scale proportionally for other time frames.
- **Weekly review** – on the last day of each week: what went well, what was hard, what to change.

## Experiments

One experiment at a time: a small, concrete action with a count and a review date, for example "Tell one interview story out loud, three times this week – review on 19 Nov". At the review, ask: did it happen, what did you notice, keep, change or drop?

## Check-in

1. Optional thought for the day – only from [Quotes](quotes.md) or a quote the user supplied. "Another quote" picks a different one; "Write about it" starts an entry.
2. Day X of Y, main focus and goal.
3. Current experiment and its review date.
4. Background journeys.
5. Optional mood word: Low, Okay, Good, Skip.
6. Pause, Skip today, Go lighter. A skipped day never needs catching up.

## Map (text form)

Show progress as a compact text grid, one column per week:

```
         W1  W2  W3  W4  W5
done     ■■■■■■■ ■■■□□ ...
today    day 12
review   day 16 (experiment), day 30 (finish)
```

Plus one line per journey with a simple text bar, for example `Career ████░░░░░░ main focus`.

## Programme record format

Saved at `[journal folder]/programme/storytime-programme.md`:

```markdown
# Storytime programme
- Started: 2026-11-04
- Time frame: 30 days (ends 2026-12-03)
- Main focus: Career and freelance
- Goal: "Update my CV and apply for five roles"
- Background journeys: Learning and getting better; Money and finances
- Check-in rhythm: 3× a week
- Last activity: 2026-11-15 – Career – drafting cover letter, Procurement Lead

## Experiments
- 2026-11-12 – Tell one interview story out loud 3× this week – review 2026-11-19 – status: 1 of 3

## Check-ins
- 2026-11-15 – day 12 – mood: Okay – note: "froze on one question"
```

All dates and contents in this example are synthetic.
